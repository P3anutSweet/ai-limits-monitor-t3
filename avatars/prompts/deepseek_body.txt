Character: a cute food-loving whale maid girl. Black-and-navy maid dress with a white frilled apron; the apron pocket is embroidered with a small pattern of three tiny white rice grains and a light-blue wave line (NO whale, NO fish, NO animal on the apron); white cuffs and collar, a black bow tie with a small blue gem. A dark blue whale tail with a pale underside curling behind her. She holds a big bowl of steaming white rice with both hands. Cute, food-loving, a bit lazy.
