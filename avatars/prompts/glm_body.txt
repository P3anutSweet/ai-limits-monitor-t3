Character: GLM (Zhipu) black fox girl: long straight black hair with blunt bangs, black fox ears with white inner fur, a big fluffy black fox tail with a white tip, thin round black glasses, grey eyes, calm sly little smile; black-and-white gothic maid dress with a high white lace collar and black ribbons, white maid headdress; she holds a closed dark hardcover book with a plain silver zigzag lightning emblem (no letters). Clever, studious.
